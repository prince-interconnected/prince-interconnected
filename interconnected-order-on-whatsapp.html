Common questions

Are the samples really free?

Yes — sample pieces of syringes, needles, IV cannula and IV sets are free so your nurses and doctors can test the quality. Choose “Request Samples” in Step 1, tap Sample on the items you want, and send. Prince will confirm the sample delivery on WhatsApp.

Why are there no prices on this page?

Hospital prices depend on quantity, contract length and delivery location, so every buyer gets an official itemised quotation on WhatsApp first — valid for 15 days. You never pay anything from this page, and nothing is confirmed until your hospital issues a Purchase Order.

What is the minimum for a trial order?

A trial order is sized for about 2 weeks of use in one department — for example a ward, ER, ICU or a specialty like Neurology or Internal Medicine. Tell Prince your department’s usual consumption in the notes, and the quotation will be sized to match.

How does a 3, 6 or 12-month contract work?

You agree a monthly quantity per product. Interconnected delivers on schedule each month under one supply agreement, with prices as stated in your signed quotation. It removes monthly re-ordering and protects you from running out of ward consumables.

Where do you deliver?

Interconnected is based in Solano, Nueva Vizcaya and serves hospitals and clinics across Region II (Nueva Vizcaya, Isabela, Cagayan, Quirino) and elsewhere in Luzon. Delivery time and any delivery charge are stated clearly in your quotation before you confirm.

I’m a clinic, pharmacy or school — can I order too?

Yes. Private clinics, diagnostic centres, pharmacies, school clinics and rural health units can request samples, trial orders and contracts the same way. Just write your type of facility in the notes.
